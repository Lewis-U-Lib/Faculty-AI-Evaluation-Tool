Welcome
Your Guide to the
AI Evaluation Tool Reference
This interactive reference is your companion to the AI Evaluation Tool. It houses all 32 AI tools, 1,131 activity ideas, and 153 active scholarly sources in one searchable, browseable interface, with counts pulled from the live page dataset. 💡 Swipe or use the arrows to walk through each feature.
🧭
Step 1 of 6
Navigate with the Command Hub
Below the header you'll find three large navigation buttons: 🛠️ Tools, 📋 Ideas, and 📚 Sources. Click any button to switch between the AI tool catalog, the full idea library, and the scholarly bibliography. As you scroll, the sticky bar at the top displays which section you're currently viewing — it updates automatically when you switch sections. 💡 The indicator bubble in the sticky bar always shows your current section: Tools, Ideas, or Sources.
🔍
Step 2 of 6
Search Everything
The large search bar in the hub lets you instantly filter across all tools, activities, and descriptions. As you scroll, a compact search field in the sticky bar stays available — both search inputs stay synced. Searching opens both the Tools and Ideas sections simultaneously so you can see results from each. 💡 Try searching a keyword like "rubric," "ethics," or a tool name like "Claude."
🛠️
Step 3 of 6
Explore AI Tool Cards
The Tools tab displays all 32 AI tools organized into categories (General-Purpose, Research, Writing, Image Generation, etc.). Each card shows the tool's description, its pricing badge, and tags describing its best use-cases. Click a tool name to visit the tool's website. 💡 Tags like "discussion," "assessment," and "feedback" help you find tools by pedagogical function.
📋
Step 4 of 6
Browse 1,131 Ideas
The Ideas tab groups all 1,131 activity ideas by category. Click a category header to expand it and reveal the ideas inside. Each idea card shows its time estimate, Bloom's level, and associated AI tools. Click any idea card to reveal its full implementation instructions. 💡 Use search to narrow down ideas across all categories at once — categories auto-expand to show matches.
📚
Step 5 of 6
Consult the Bibliography
The Sources tab contains all 153 active scholarly references that informed the tool. Sources marked with a ✦ gold star are directly linked to individual ideas through the tool's provenance system. 💡 Look for the 🔗 icon next to entries for direct links to the original publication.
🎯
Step 6 of 6
Return to the Evaluation Tool
This Reference Guide is your map — everything in one browseable, searchable view. The AI Evaluation Tool is your guide — it uses a transparent, weighted rules engine to match your specific teaching context (discipline, task, Bloom's level, time) to the most relevant tools and activities from this same dataset. The Tool also includes a filter bar (All · Lower-stakes · Teaching · Faculty) to quickly narrow results by context, and a ☆ Save feature that lets you shortlist and export your favorite activity ideas across sessions. Use the "← Back to Tool" button in the top navigation — or the link at the bottom of the page — to return to the main interactive tool. 💡 The Tool and this Reference Guide share the same data — they're two views of the same resource. Start here to explore; go there to get matched.
